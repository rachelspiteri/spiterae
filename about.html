I’m Rae Spiteri, an animator and illustrator, specializing in concept develpment and artistic direction. I explore surreal themes and the human experience through strange scenes with strange characters.

Over the years, I’ve developed a love for stop-motion and producing miniatures, as in my short film ‘End of World’. I'm interested in creating whimsical worlds with a dash of absurdism, using both analogue and digital methods.

I draw inspiration from organic forms, sometimes seeping into abstraction. Visual aesthetics are fundamental to my works, all containing their own intricacies which relate to the fragility of human consciousness.
