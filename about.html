hi!

I’m Rae Spiteri, a Maltese animator and illustrator based in Netherlands, and I specialize in concept develpment and artistic direction. I enjoy exploring surreal themes and the human experience through strange scenes with strange characters.

Over the years, I’ve developed a love for stop-motion and producing miniatures, as in my short film ‘End of World’. I'm interested in creating whimsical worlds with a dash of absurdism, using both analogue and digital methods.

I draw inspiration from organic forms, sometimes seeping into abstraction. Visual aesthetics are fundamental to my works, each containing their own intricacies mirroring the fragility of human consciousness.
