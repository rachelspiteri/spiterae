who nose?
(visual design)
What happens when self-perception replaces self-awareness? How much of us is invented from the world around us?

"A vain rental agent wakes up to find his nose cut off.
He goes on a humiliating journey through an indifferent and busy city to hunt for the man who did this to him.
As his detective journey unfolds, our main character is forced to confront the absurdity of the urban power structures that he has privileged from all his life."

Director: Ana Daskalova




Who nose? is less a mystery to be solved than an experience to be lived. It is a story about lack of authenticity, and the role of the modern life in shaping our identity.

As the visual designer for this pitch, I was very eager to experiment with design choices and techniques and find the most representative way of visually narrating the story. The visual language, which uses a mixed media technique through both 2d and 3d animation, recreates the confusing internal experience of the main character.

The story "The Nose" by Nikolaj Gogol is an ideal source material with its satirical view on society and its existential absurdity, themes that remain relevant today.
