end of world (2024)

A cryptic creature with a cryptic message.

This short stop-motion animation follows a nihilistic little creature who has come to terms with their own fate, sitting and waiting for their fragile world to collapse.

This was my first project, which I put into motion on my own in my brother's bedroom. It came from a deep place of love, and I still hold a lot of passion for this world I built.
