organic matter (2024)

This animation deals with themes of loss and grief and aims to reframe death as a continuation of life.

To honor someones life we should honor the ground they came from.
I like to believe that passed ones never really leave (which is technically true) but become a part of something bigger. I also sought to promote the idea of natural burials, where one can be taken in naturally by living ecosystems.

Dying alone doesn’t mean going to waste. Nature teaches us something about community, how to support and provide for the other. By sustaining the idea of this community in life, I want to reject the idea of solitude in death.
