organic matter (2024)

This animation deals with themes of loss and grief and aims to reframe death as a continuation of life.

To honor someones life we should honor the ground they came from.
I want to approach death from a more hopeful standpoint, as a tool for grief. I also sought to promote the idea of natural burials, where one can be taken in naturally by living ecosystems.
I like to believe that passed ones never really leave, which is technically true, they become a part of something bigger.
But in cases of green burials one can be buried in shroud or raw wood and be taken by living ecosystem around them. This is possible in over 25 cemetries in Netherlands alone.
Dying alone doesn’t mean going to waste. Nature teaches us something about community, how to support and provide for the other. By sustaining the idea of this community in life, I want to reject the idea of solitude in death.
